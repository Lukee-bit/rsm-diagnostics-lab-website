Four methods on one stool specimen

PCR, culture, Total Parasites via Microscopy, and intestinal markers run on the same collection so infection, inflammation, and digestive function can be read on one report.

PCR and culture, not one or the other

Multiplex PCR detects targets at the genetic level. Culture shows which organisms are viable, adding context that helps separate clinically relevant findings from incidental molecular detections.

Total Parasites via Microscopy (Gold Standard)

Direct visualization of protozoa and helminths, reported in addition to PCR. Microscopy can improve parasite detection and organism characterization that molecular panels alone can miss.

Sensitivity data you can prescribe from

Positive bacterial and fungal cultures carry antibiotic susceptibility plus sensitivity against a panel of natural agents, giving integrative providers a fuller set of options.

Total GI vs the competition

Broader pathogen detection, Total Parasites via Microscopy (Gold Standard), culture-confirmed sensitivity, and a deeper functional marker menu. See how Total GI measures up against the GI panels providers most often compare it to.
