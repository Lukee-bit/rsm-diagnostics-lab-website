RSM Diagnostics Lab LLC (“RSM”) is a CLIA-certified high-complexity clinical laboratory. Use the sections below to review the official CMS CLIA Certificate of Compliance and the CMS Survey & Certification QCOR laboratory details associated with CLIA ID 08D2253750. For the National Provider Identifier (NPI) record, see NPI 1770236127 on the CMS NPPES Registry.

An NPI is a provider identifier only. An NPI record does not establish or verify CLIA certification, laboratory licensure, accreditation, payer participation, network status, credentialing, clinical capability, test availability, or compliance status.

Information on this page is provided for general informational purposes only and may be incomplete, delayed, inaccurate, or out of date. RSM does not warrant the accuracy, completeness, current status, validity, expiration date, or continued availability of any credential, certificate, registry entry, external link, or third-party record displayed or referenced on this page. For official verification, rely only on the current record, certificate, and information available directly from the applicable government agency, registry, or accrediting body.

RSM’s CLIA certificate applies only to the laboratory, location, certificate type, and effective period identified in the applicable official CMS record, subject to all applicable legal, regulatory, licensure, quality, personnel, and testing requirements. It does not by itself establish that a particular test, test panel, specimen type, collection method, service area, billing arrangement, provider, patient, payer, or order is available, covered, reimbursable, authorized, medically necessary, or compliant with all applicable requirements.
